## Task

Research three areas of data work and prepare a short group presentation explaining:

1. The role of a **data engineer**.
2. The role of a **data analyst**.
3. The role of a **data scientist**.

Each person should explain which role or roles they see themselves doing. The presentation should also discuss the skills required and the skills already held. The trainer left room for the group to choose how to organise that skills comparison.

## Requirements recorded from the session

- Cover all three roles within the five-minute presentation.
- Include an individual role preference from every group member.
- Discuss required skills and existing skills.
- Choose a format that communicates the ideas clearly; slides were suggested.
- Make a start on Day 1 and prepare to present the following day. Additional preparation time could be provided on Day 2.

Research into other job titles was optional and outside the minimum required scope.
